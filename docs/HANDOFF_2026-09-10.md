## Implemented and intentionally excluded

Implemented (all fixture-path, UI-layer only):

- Cart: line sanitization on read, quantity cap (99), deduplication, NaN/negative guards,
  safe-integer Rial math (`lineTotalRials`/`computeCartTotals` throw on overflow), and a
  `MAX_CART_QUANTITY` constant.
- Privacy: `CartProvider` and `OrderProvider` scope localStorage keys per
  `principal.userId`, with an explicit `guest` scope for anonymous users.
- Iranian input: new `lib/iran.ts` — Persian/Arabic-Indic digit normalization, mobile forms
  `09…`, `+98…`, `0098…`, 10-digit postal code with non-zero checks, canonical 31-province list.
- Checkout: province `<select>`, mobile/postal normalization, `autoComplete` hints, form-level
  `aria` wiring, note persisted on the order, idempotency key generated at first submit
  (per mount), normalized values sent to the port.
- Payment: cart is retained until `markPaid` succeeds (removed per-item from the cart after
  success); pay failures surface an alert and allow retry; the old “cart cleared then
  payment failed” bug is gone.
- Orders: `/orders` requires an authenticated principal (fail in UI), notes render on the
  detail page, fixture validates item/totals/shipping input and rejects manipulated totals,
  invalid Iranian contact data, duplicate product lines and oversize notes.
- Dates: `formatTimestamp` renders in `Asia/Tehran` before the Jalali conversion.

Intentionally excluded (explicitly NOT faked, per FOUNDATION/DEV_PLAN §0.4–0.5 and
EXECUTION_STATUS "Explicitly not ready"):

- Server-side repricing, availability recheck and reservations — backend-owned; the client
  never claims authority.
- A real Iranian payment gateway, verified callback, refunds and reconciliation.
- Server-owned idempotency: the client `idempotencyKey` is a fixture-local dedupe only; the
  backend must own the durable key/header contract.
- Guest-vs-account cart merge and cart expiry policy (needs A decision, ready-queue item 8).
- Shipping quote service and address CRUD/ownership (needs A decision).
